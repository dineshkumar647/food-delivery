FOODIEGO — FOOD DELIVERY WEBSITE

How to run:
1. Download and extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the FoodieGo folder.
3. Double-click index.html.
4. Use Chrome, Edge or Firefox.

Features:
- Browse six sample restaurants.
- Open a restaurant and view its food menu.
- Filter dishes by category.
- Add items to cart.
- Increase/decrease item quantity and remove items.
- View subtotal, delivery fee and total.
- Checkout form with name, phone, address and payment method.
- Order confirmation with a demo order number.
- Responsive design for mobile and desktop.

Important:
This is a front-end demo made with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. It does not connect to real restaurants,
payment services, delivery partners or a server. Orders are demo-only and no real payment or delivery
will happen. For a real online food delivery service, connect a backend, database, authentication,
restaurant dashboard and a payment gateway.
